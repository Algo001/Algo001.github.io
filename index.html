OpenClaw Assistant

A personal, single-user automation assistant.

OpenClaw Assistant is a private tool built and operated by one person for their own use. It runs on a private server and helps its owner keep track of university deadlines, work shifts, appointments and personal admin.

What it does

  • Reads forwarded university email and extracts dates, deadlines and required actions
  • Creates matching tasks and calendar entries for its owner
  • Sends the owner a short summary when something needs attention

Who can use it

Nobody else. There is no sign-up, no other users, and no public interface. It authenticates as its owner and acts only on that one account.

Google data

The app requests access to the owner's Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Tasks and Contacts in order to do the above. It never sends email, posts, or shares anything publicly without an explicit instruction from its owner. See the privacy policy for details on how that data is handled.
