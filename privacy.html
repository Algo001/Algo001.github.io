Privacy Policy

OpenClaw Assistant · last updated 26 September 2026

OpenClaw Assistant is a private, single-user tool. It is operated by one individual for their own personal use. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no public interface. This policy describes how that one person's own Google data is handled by the software running on their private server.

What data is accessed

ScopeWhy
Gmail (read, modify, send)Read forwarded university email to extract deadlines and required actions. Send is not used for automated mail.
CalendarRead existing commitments and add time blocks for extracted deadlines.
TasksCreate and update the owner's task list.
Drive, Sheets, DocsRead and write the owner's own planning documents.
Contacts (read-only)Recognise senders when triaging mail.

Where the data goes

  • Data is processed on a private server controlled solely by the owner.
  • Email text and attached images may be sent to a third-party AI model provider for the sole purpose of extracting dates and actions. That provider processes the content to return a result and is not permitted to use it for anything else.
  • Nothing is sold, published, shared with advertisers, or disclosed to any other person or organisation.
  • No data from this app is used to train AI models by the operator.

What is stored

Extracted details (subject, sender, dates, required action) and downloaded image attachments are kept locally on the owner's server so the same message is not processed twice. Image attachments are deleted automatically after 14 days. Google credentials are stored on that server with restricted file permissions and are never transmitted elsewhere.

What the app will not do

  • Send or reply to email automatically
  • Delete, archive or mark messages as read
  • Contact any third party on the owner's behalf without explicit instruction
  • Expose credentials, tokens or private links

Retention and removal

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends all access. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time, and image attachments expire automatically after 14 days.

Limited Use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
