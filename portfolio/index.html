Kin

What it is

An app that helps you keep in touch with the people who matter. It reads your message history and notices things, like when you and a friend are slowly drifting apart.

What I did
  • Invented my own method using math and statistics, instead of paying for an AI service.
  • Taught it everyday common sense by combining five public language databases into one.
  • Tested it on 16,000 real messages and wrote a research paper on how it works.

Hermes

What it is

My own AI assistant that I text on Telegram. It reads my school emails, puts events in my calendar and runs daily jobs on its own, running on a server I set up myself.

What I did
  • Set up and run the server myself, with the assistant, its dashboard and a small AI model.
  • Noticed it was getting slow and expensive, and investigated why.
  • Cut about half of the wasted steps from its daily email job and taught it to read pictures.
★ The impressive part

I found a hidden leak. The assistant was re-reading 75 days of old chat every time I sent a message, using about 5× more than the reports showed. One fix stopped it.

UW Portal

What it is

One phone app for student life: homework due dates with reminders, grades and GPA, and a 3D map of campus.

What I did
